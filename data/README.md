# 📡 Raw Data Collection

The drone collects **raw video data** of photovoltaic (PV) panels during its inspection.  
These videos are crucial for **training** and **evaluating detection models**.  
The raw data includes both **thermal** and **RGB footage** captured at various altitudes.
